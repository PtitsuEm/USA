# SelectUSA Contact Review

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-15  
**Scope:** All 56 `selectusa-*` rows in `data/contacts.csv`.  
**Method:** Current official SelectUSA, government, agency, or employer pages only. The SelectUSA directory establishes that it publishes a jurisdictional investment contact, but by itself does not establish that the named person remains employed. Email, phone, and LinkedIn were assessed independently. A syntactically valid email or NANP-shaped phone number is not proof that an inbox or line works; no MX/DNS result, guessed email, LinkedIn search URL, or unlinked individual LinkedIn profile was accepted.

## Status Key

- **Recommend `verified`:** a current official employer or agency page independently confirms the individual, role, and agency; direct contacts are retained only when that page publishes them.
- **Recommend `needs_review`:** SelectUSA supports a current jurisdictional office contact, but an individual employer/agency confirmation was not available or a discrepancy needs resolution.
- **Recommend `replace/remove`:** current official employer/agency evidence conflicts with the row and identifies a replacement. No such recommendation was necessary in this pass.
